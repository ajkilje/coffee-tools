Flour & Feine is an open-source collection of coffee brewing tools for coffee enthusiasts, home brewers, and professionals.

Features include:

• Brew Ratio Calculator
• Water calculation
• Brewing recommendations
• Offline support
• Progressive Web App
• Native Android app

Supported brewing methods:

• V60
• Pour Over
• French Press
• AeroPress
• AeroPress (Inverted)
• Moka Pot
• Cold Brew

Coffee Tools is the first collection of utilities available within the Flour & Feine app.
